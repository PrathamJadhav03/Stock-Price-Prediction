## Data Flow

1. **Ingestion**: Raw stock CSV files are loaded from `Data/` via Pandas into DataFrames with date indexing.
2. **Exploration & Cleaning**:
   - Descriptive statistics (`describe()`, `info()`, `isna().sum()`) identify data properties.
   - Missing records in IBM dataset are pruned via `dropna()`.
   - Feature distributions and time-series trends are visualized using Seaborn, Matplotlib, and Plotly.
3. **Preprocessing & Sequence Generation**:
   - The univariate `'Close'` price is extracted and normalized to the range `[-1, 1]` via Scikit-learn's `MinMaxScaler`.
   - The `split_data` routine slices the continuous series into overlapping windows of `lookback = 20` historical timesteps.
   - The temporal sequence is partitioned chronologically into 80% training set and 20% testing set.
   - Arrays are converted to PyTorch FloatTensors: `x_train` shape `(N, 19, 1)`, `y_train` shape `(N, 1)`.
4. **Model Training**:
   - PyTorch `GRU(input_dim=1, hidden_dim=32, num_layers=2, output_dim=1)` is instantiated.
   - Optimization runs for 105 epochs with Adam (`lr=0.01`) minimizing Mean Squared Error (`MSELoss`).
5. **Inference & Rescaling**:
   - Forward pass predictions on test tensors `x_test` are detached and converted back to NumPy arrays.
   - `scaler.inverse_transform` converts normalized predictions back to real USD price scales.
6. **Evaluation & Visualization**:
   - RMSE is computed comparing actual vs. predicted values for both train and test splits.
   - Training loss curves and comparison charts are rendered via Matplotlib and Plotly Graph Objects.
